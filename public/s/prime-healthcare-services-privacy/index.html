Who's in it

Settlement Class is defined to mean all patients of Prime Healthcare and the Relevant Hospitals that accessed and/or utilized any of the Prime Web Properties during the period from November 1, 2019, through the date all tracking pixels were definitively removed from the respective Prime Web Property for each such Relevant Hospital, as reflected in Appendix A.

Patient Portal Subclass

Settlement Class Members who accessed one of Prime Healthcare’s patient portals between November 1, 2019, and the Class Period End Date for the relevant Prime Web Property, which you may review at Question 5 (the Patient Portal Subclass)

Email Subclass

individuals who were patients at one of Prime Healthcare’s California hospitals between November 1, 2021, and the Class Period End Date for the relevant California hospital, which you may review at Question 5, and who provided Prime Healthcare an email address (the Email Subclass)
