What happened

On or around May 18, 2025, an unauthorized actor gained access to the Defendants’ IT environment, potentially compromising patients’ personally identifiable information (“PII”) and private health information (“PHI”) collectively, “Private Information,” (the “Data Incident”).

Data involved: names, addresses, dates of birth, medical record numbers, Social Security numbers, treatment information, health insurance information.
