Who's in it

All natural persons residing in the United States who made payment card purchases on e-commerce websites operated by or on behalf of Defendant’s subsidiaries between April 25, 2020 and August 5, 2020.

From the official site. You decide whether it describes you.

California Subclass

All members of the Settlement Class who were residents of the State of California between April 25, 2020 and August 5, 2020, and who made payment card purchases on e-commerce websites operated by or on behalf of Defendant’s subsidiaries during that time period.

What it pays

Pro rata cash payment, estimated $50 per Settlement Class Member; no documentation needed.
Official wording“The estimated weighted pro rata Cash Claim Payment is $50 for Settlement Class Members and $100 for California Subclass Members.”
California Subclass members get a double share, estimated $100.
Official wording“California Subclass Members will receive one (1) additional share (for a total of two (2) shares) per valid claim.”

Total fund: $1,250,000.

What happened

Plaintiffs allege that the cybersecurity incident resulted in unauthorized third parties gaining access to consumer payment information associated with purchases made on the affected websites between April 25, 2020 and August 5, 2020.

Data involved: payment card information.
