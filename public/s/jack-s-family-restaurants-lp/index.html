What happened

This class action lawsuit concerned the targeted cyberattack on Jack’s computer systems that occurred approximately between July 24 – August 10, 2025 (the “Data Incident”).

Data involved: names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, bank account information for direct deposit of wages.
