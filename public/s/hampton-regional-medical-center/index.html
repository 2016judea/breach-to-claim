What happened

A Settlement has been reached with Hampton Regional Medical Center (“Defendant”) in a class action lawsuit about a cybersecurity incident that occurred between June 18 and July 16, 2025 and resulted in the unauthorized access to or acquisition of individuals’ Private Information (“Data Incident”).

Data involved: Social Security numbers, dates of birth, driver’s license or state identification numbers, medical information, other demographic information.
