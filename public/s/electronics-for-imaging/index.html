What happened

This case is about the targeted cyberattack on EFI's computer systems that occurred in September 2024 (the “Data Incident”). Certain files that contained private information were accessed.

Data involved: names, addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, photo identification and/or driver's licenses, Social Security numbers.
