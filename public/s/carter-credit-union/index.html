What happened

This case is about the targeted cyberattack on Carter Credit Union's computer systems that occurred in June 2025 (the “Data Incident”). Certain files that contained private information were accessed.

Data involved: names, addresses, Social Security Numbers, driver’s license and government-issued ID numbers, financial information, medical information, health insurance information, dates of birth.
